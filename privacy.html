🥊 Fist of Fury — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Fist of Fury ("the Game", "we", "us") is a free online multiplayer fighting game. This policy explains what data the Game accesses and how it is used. We respect your privacy and collect as little as possible.

1. Information We Collect

  • Display name & loadout: The nickname, character color, fighter and map you choose. These are shared only with players in your room during a match and are not stored on our servers after the match ends.
  • Room codes: Temporary codes used to connect two players. They exist only in memory while a room is active and are deleted when the room empties.
  • Chat messages: Text chat is relayed in real time between the two players in a room and is never logged or stored.
  • Microphone (voice chat): If you enable Voice Chat, the Game requests microphone access. Audio is sent peer-to-peer (WebRTC) directly to your opponent and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect real names, emails, phone numbers, or accounts.
  • We do not store gameplay recordings, chat logs, or voice audio.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

3. Permissions

The mobile app version may request the following device permissions:

  • Microphone — only when you enable Voice Chat, to talk to your opponent during a match.
  • Internet — required to connect to the multiplayer server and other players.

4. Children's Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no personal data is stored, the Game is suitable for general audiences. Voice chat should be supervised for younger players.

5. Data Security

All real-time communication uses encrypted WebSocket and WebRTC connections. Because we store no personal data, there is no personal database that could be breached.

6. Third-Party Services

The Game uses a public STUN server (Google) only to help establish peer-to-peer voice connections. No audio passes through it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For any privacy questions, contact the developer through your app store listing or the repository where this game is hosted.
