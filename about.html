🥊 Fist of Fury

Online Multiplayer Fighting Game

Fist of Fury is a free, browser-based fighting game you can play right now — no download, no sign-up, no installation. Challenge a friend to a real-time online duel from anywhere in the world using a simple room code, or sharpen your skills against the built-in CPU opponent. Inspired by arcade classics like Tekken and Dragon Ball FighterZ, the game combines responsive touch controls, dramatic energy-beam clashes, and a roster of 24 unique fighters across 24 hand-crafted arenas.

🎯 What Is Fist of Fury?

Fist of Fury is a 2D fighting game built entirely with modern web technology (React, TypeScript, and the HTML5 Canvas). It runs smoothly in any modern browser — on your phone, tablet, laptop, or desktop — and is also available as a native Android app for an even smoother experience on mobile. The whole game is optimized for portrait-to-landscape mobile play with on-screen touch buttons, while desktop players get full keyboard support.

The goal is simple: deplete your opponent's health bar before they deplete yours. Land punches, kicks, and throws to build your blue meter, and once it's charged past 50%, unleash your fighter's signature special beam attack — a Kamehameha-style energy blast that launches your foe across the arena.

🎮 How to Play

1. Online Multiplayer (Play With a Friend)

  1. Open the game and type your name.
  2. Tap CREATE ROOM — you'll get a 5-letter code (e.g. K7H2P).
  3. Send that code to your friend via any messenger.
  4. Your friend opens the same website, enters the code, and taps JOIN ROOM.
  5. Both players tap I'M READY — a 3-2-1 countdown begins, then FIGHT!

Rooms are strictly limited to two players. If a third person tries the same code, they'll see "Room is already full" until someone leaves.

2. Practice Mode (Play vs CPU)

If you want to practice alone or your friend isn't online, tap PLAY vs CPU. You'll fight an AI opponent that uses punches, kicks, blocks, and special beams. Watch for the ⚠ CHARGING ⚠ warning above the CPU's head — when you see it, run to the opposite corner and fire your own beam to trigger a dramatic beam clash!

⚡ The Beam Clash System

The signature mechanic of Fist of Fury. When two fighters fire their special beams at each other and the beams meet head-on, the game enters a cinematic beam clash:

  • Intro: Time slows down, shockwave rings ripple outward, and a "BEAM CLASH!" banner appears.
  • Mash phase: A glowing orb sits between the two beams. Tap PUNCH or SPECIAL as fast as you can. The more you mash, the more your beam grows and pushes the orb toward your opponent.
  • Resolve: The winner's beam surges forward and the loser's beam disintegrates into sparks, dealing big damage and launching them across the arena.

A tug-of-war bar at the top of the screen shows who's winning in real time, so you always know whether to mash harder. In online play the result is server-authoritative — both players always see the same outcome.

🔋 Graphics Quality: High vs Low Mode

Not all phones are equally powerful. That's why Fist of Fury has a built-in graphics quality toggle (the ⚡ HIGH / 🔋 LOW button in the top-right corner of the fight screen).

⚡ HIGH Mode (Default)

Full visual effects: spiraling beam helixes, travelling energy rings, lightning sparks, particle glow, and smooth blur. Best for modern phones (e.g. Samsung Galaxy S20 FE, Pixel 6, iPhone 13+) and desktop computers.

🔋 LOW Mode (For budget phones)

Disables the most GPU-intensive effects (beam spirals, rings, lightning, particle blur) and reduces the particle cap from 220 to 80. The game still looks great and plays identically, but runs at a smooth frame rate on weaker hardware like the Samsung Galaxy A22, Redmi Note series, or older devices. Your setting is saved automatically — set it once and it sticks.

Tip: If you experience lag during beam clashes, switch to LOW mode.

🌐 Do I Need an Internet Connection?

🎮 vs CPU — Works Offline

Play vs CPU mode runs entirely on your device. Once the game has loaded in your browser, you can play against the AI with no internet connection at all.

🛡️ Online Multiplayer — Needs Internet & a Live Server

Create Room and Join Room require an active internet connection because they connect to the game server (hosted on Render). The server handles room codes, matchmaking, and relays the fight data between the two players in real time. Without the server running, online mode will show "Can't reach the game server."

⏱️ Why Does the First Load Take a Few Seconds?

The game is hosted on Render's free plan. Free Render services sleep after ~15 minutes of inactivity to save resources. When you (or your friend) visit the game after it's been sleeping, the server takes 30–50 seconds to wake up on the very first request. You'll see a brief loading state — just wait, and it connects normally. After that, every subsequent visit is fast until the server goes idle again.

This is normal behavior for free-tier cloud hosting and not a bug. Once both players are connected and in a room, the real-time fight stays smooth.

💰 Ads & Monetization (How This Game Stays Free)

Fist of Fury is completely free to play. To keep it that way, the game is supported by ads — but ads are placed responsibly and in line with Google's policies:

  • On the website (this page): Google AdSense display ads may appear. These only load in a real browser — never inside the Android app.
  • In the Android app: Google AdMob banner and interstitial ads. AdMob is the correct ad network for mobile apps (AdSense is not used inside the app, per Google's policy).
  • During gameplay: No ads are shown while you're actively fighting. Ads only appear on menus (lobby / waiting room) and between matches.

🔐 Privacy

Fist of Fury does not require an account and stores no personal data. Room codes and chat messages live only in the server's memory while a room is active and are deleted the moment the room empties. Voice chat is peer-to-peer (WebRTC) — your audio goes directly to the other player and never passes through the server. Read the full Privacy Policy.

🎧 Voice Chat Tip: Always use headphones or earphones when enabling voice chat to avoid echo and feedback. The game will ask for microphone permission the first time you turn it on — tap Allow.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play on iPhone?

Yes — the web version works in Safari on iPhone and iPad. An iOS app is not yet available, but the browser version is fully featured.

My friend can't join my room. What's wrong?

Make sure the game server is awake (see the cold-start note above), that both of you are using the exact same 5-letter code, and that neither of you has a third player already in the room. Rooms are strictly two-player.

The game feels laggy on my phone. What can I do?

Switch to 🔋 LOW graphics mode using the button in the top-right of the fight screen. This disables the heaviest visual effects and keeps beam clashes smooth on budget devices.

Does the beam clash look the same on both players' screens?

Yes. In online play the clash is coordinated by the server and the result is authoritative — both players always see the same winner and the same dramatic disintegration.

Is Fist of Fury really free?

Yes, 100% free with no pay-to-win mechanics. Ads only appear on menus and between matches, never during active gameplay.
